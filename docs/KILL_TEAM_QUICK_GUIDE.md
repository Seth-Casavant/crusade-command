# KILL TEAM QUICK GUIDE

Once your Kill Team is registered, you only need one command:

`/crusade-submit`

The bot automatically identifies your Kill Team.

Choose what you are submitting:

- Mission Completion
- Terminus Kill
- Objective Completion

Then upload the required screenshot.

The bot confirms the submission and updates your Kill Team's Crusade progress
automatically.

IMPORTANT:

- You do not need to select your Kill Team manually.
- You cannot edit campaign data directly.
- Mission Completion can only be credited once.
- Terminus kills require a screenshot for each valid submission.
- Only submit evidence for the ACTIVE Crusade mission.
